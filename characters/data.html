“Childhood”
c. 2336
Data's construction completed
Dr. Noonien Soong completed construction of Data around 2336. Soong and his wife, Juliana, cared for Data's early existence, helping Data navigate motor control and sensory input as his neural nets formed. After some time, Soong decided to download the logs and journals of the Omicron Theta colonists into Data, while also wiping Data's memory of his earliest years.
Early Life
2338
Data activated on Omicron Theta
After the Crystalline Entity destroyed all life on planet Omicron Theta, Data was found and reactivated by Starfleet officers from the USS Tripoli. He retained no memory of his earlier upbringing.

2341
Data admitted to Starfleet Academy
Because of his connection to the Starfleet officers who found him, Data chose to apply to Starfleet Academy. Data was accepted into the Academy despite Bruce Maddox being “the only member of the evaluation committee to oppose [Data's] entrance on the grounds that [he] was not a sentient being”.
2345
Data graduates from the Academy, is assigned to the USS Trieste
Data graduated with honors in exobiology and probability mechanics. He was assigned to the USS Trieste where he spent three years as an ensign and twelve as a lieutenant.
2348
Data promoted to lieutenant
Presumably, he was promoted to lieutenant j.g. and then later to a full lieutenant, but the exact dates are not known.

Service on the USS Enterprise‑D
2364
Data visits the site of his own discovery on Omicron Theta
After being assigned to the USS Enterprise‑D earlier in the year as its second officer, he and the crew revisited the site on Omicron Theta where Data was first discovered. There, they found an underground lab with another android, disassembled, that looked just like Data.

c. 2386
Altan Soong & Bruce Maddox create the Coppelius androids
In or around this year, using a method called fractal neuronic cloning, Noonien Soong's son creates several new androids – offspring of Data – with one of Data's positronic neurons.
2399
Star Trek: Picard Season 1 Spoiler »
Data's consciousness encounters Picard in a complex quantum simulation
A version of Data's consciousness existed based on Data's memories that were uploaded to the B-4 android, which were revived in a quantum simulation by Soong's neuronic cloning process. While Picard's memories were being uploaded to Soong's golem, Data's consciousness was able to interact with that of Picard's within the simulation. This version of Data made one last request of his long-time friend: to terminate his consciousness so that he could appreciate the finite nature of Human life. After leaving the simulation, Picard complied with Data's request.
PIC 1x10: Et in Arcadia Ego, Part 2
Star Trek: Picard Season 3 Spoiler »
Altan Soong constructs a new android combining parts of B-4, Lore, Lal, and Data
With the lifting of the ban on synths, Altan Soong continued his life's work by creating a new android, combining the memories and personalities of several of its predecessors. Soong died before completing his project, and his android was seized by Section 31 and made to serve as an AI security system at Daystrom Station.
Star Trek: Picard Season 3
2401
Star Trek: Picard Season 3 Spoiler »
Data triumphs over Lore in an internal battle for control of the android body
After being rescued from Daystrom Station, the new Soong android was unstable, with the Data and Lore personalities at odds with each other. After a struggle for dominance, Data was the victor. This “new” Data now had a more-human personality, influenced by Lore's memories, and he went on to play a crucial role in the Enterprise‑D's victory over one final Borg threat against the Federation.
