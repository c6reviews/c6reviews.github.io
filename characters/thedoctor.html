Service on the USS Voyager
2371
The Doctor's first two activations
The Doctor was first briefly activated while Voyager was still in drydock by Admiral Patterson. Janeway was not impressed with The Doctor's personality.
The Doctor was then activated for a second time after Voyager was pulled into the Delta Quadrant and the Chief Medical Officer was killed. He was immediately inundated with several injured crewmembers.

VOY 1x01/02: Caretaker
2372
The Doctor faces an existential crisis
When his program malfunctions due to a Kinoplasmic radiation surge, The Doctor can't figure out if he's a human or a hologram.
VOY 2x03: Projections
The Doctor must consider a full reset after his program starts to degrade
The Doctor's program becomes overloaded with all of the new skills and activities he's been undertaking, and his only options are a full reset or to trust in a cranky diagnostic program that looks just like him.
VOY 3x04: The Swarm
2373
The Doctor gains freedom with a new mobile emitter
During a time-traveling adventure, The Doctor obtains a 29th-century “mobile emitter” that allows him to travel autonomously instead of being confined to sickbay or a holodeck.
VOY 3x09: Future's End, Part II
2374
The Doctor travels to the Alpha Quadrant and makes contact with Starfleet
Using an ancient alien communications relay, The Doctor's program is transferred from the Delta Quadrant to a Starfleet vessel on the edge of Federation space.
VOY 4x14: Message in a Bottle
2375
The Doctor deals with a feedback loop in his ethical programming
When a mystery resurfaces about a surgery The Doctor doesn't remember performing, his program is thrown into chaos as his ethical subroutines fail to cope with a decision previously erased from his memory.
VOY 5x11: Latent Image
2375
Voyager crash‑lands on an ice‑covered planet
After an unsuccessful attempt to use quantum slipstream technology, Voyager crashes and all hands are lost except for Harry Kim and Chakotay. The Doctor's program remained offline for many years.

The Doctor experiments with daydreaming

The Doctor writes a holo-novel
After writing a holo-novel about his own struggles as a hologram, The Doctor fights a legal battle over the rights to his own work, and strikes the first blow for the rights of holograms.

2391–3072

c.3074
A backup copy of The Doctor is activated at the Museum of Kyrian Heritage
700 years prior in 2374, The Doctor's backup module is lost during a conflict between the Kyrians and the Vaskans. It was later found and restored by a Kyrian scientist. This copy of The Doctor had no memory since 700 years prior, and he found himself having to right the record of Voyager's involvement in the conflict. It was said that the Doctor stayed for many years, but eventually took a small ship and headed for Earth. It's unknown what became of him since.
